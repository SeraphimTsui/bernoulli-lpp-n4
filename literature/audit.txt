CURRENT PRIMARY SOURCE
Sam McKeown and Shuta Nakajima, On the most likely geodesic in last passage percolation, arXiv:2608.11378v2, 20 September 2026.
Version page: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11378
Inspected text: https://arxiv.org/html/2608.11378v2
Version history: v1 11 August 2026; latest listed v2 20 September 2026. Section 1 defines geodesics by membership in the maximizing set. Section 2, subsection Monotonicity of path probabilities, specifies signed runs and bump/unwind moves; Figures 3/4 give geometric interpretations. The unnumbered expectation immediately before Theorem 3 motivates the present target. Theorem 3, equation (6), proves strict increase only for sufficiently small positive p. Section 5, Lemmas 6–9 and proof of Theorem 3, uses two-dot counts and bounds the remaining terms. Its explicit choice gives p_4=1/18400. It supplies no all-p n=4 count table in the inspected material. Remark 1 concerns distribution-dependent reversal between some path pairs, not a certificate for every ordered Bernoulli pair. Figure 1 is exponential-weight simulation, not the target calculation. The v2 note and Appendix C resolve a different former moderate-deviation assumption; that old v1 condition is excluded from this project.
