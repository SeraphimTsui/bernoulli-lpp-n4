Date: 2026-10-29 (Asia/Shanghai)
Scope: n=1,2,3,4 only; all configurations, all paths, all maximizing ties.
Author/reviewer status: independently implemented by an AI subagent; no human peer review and no proof-assistant formal verification.

Method and independence
The script src/replay_dp.js was written without reading the main enumerator's key comparison code or importing its path data. Its only shared specification is the mathematical model, row-major free-vertex order, and lexicographic path-word convention R<U. It recursively generates all up/right words and stores lists of grid indices. Each configuration is unpacked into individual 0/1 grid entries. The maximum is found by the row-major lattice recurrence
  D(x,y) = w(x,y) + max(D(x-1,y),D(x,y-1)),
with the unique available predecessor on the boundary. For each word, its grid entries are added directly and compared to D(n,n). The code has no path-mask population count or path-derived maximum. Every equality increments that path's integer counter. Uint32 counters are sufficient since the complete configuration count is at most 8,388,608 < 2^32. Grid scores are at most 7 for n=4 after endpoints are removed, and fit the Uint8 arrays.

Endpoint omission
Every path contains both endpoints; replacing their weights by zero subtracts the same quantity from every path score and therefore preserves all maximizing ties. The free-bit labelling is row-major y then x with those two cells removed. All intermediate row-major cells are consecutive, so grid index = bit index+1.

Complete replay coverage
n=1: 4 configurations, 2 paths; n=2: 128 configurations, 6 paths;
n=3: 16,384 configurations, 20 paths; n=4: 8,388,608 configurations, 70 paths.
Each configuration is visited exactly once by the integer loop [0,2^m). Every configuration is required to have at least one maximizing word, and no word may exceed the DP optimum.

Hand derivation for all n=2 symmetry classes
Let the seven free vertex weights be a=w(1,0), b=w(2,0), c=w(0,1), d=w(1,1), e=w(2,1), f=w(0,2), g=w(1,2). The six path weights are
  RRUU: a+b+e; RURU: a+d+e; RUUR: a+d+g;
  URRU: c+d+e; URUR: c+d+g; UURR: c+f+g.
Use the ordinary counting variable t: G_word(t)=sum_configuration 1{word is maximal} t^(number of ones). Define Q=1+t+t^2 (an ordered binary pair x>=y), and E=1+t^2 (an equal binary pair). Once G is obtained, the Bernoulli probability is (1-p)^7 G(p/(1-p)) for 0<=p<1, with continuous extension at 1.

Staircase RURU.
Necessary comparisons give d>=b, e>=g, a>=c. These imply the remaining middle-path comparison. The remaining corner comparison is a+d+e>=c+f+g, or d+(a-c)+(e-g)>=f. Under the three ordered-pair constraints, this fails exactly when d=b=0, a=c, e=g, and f=1. Therefore
  G_RURU=(1+t)Q^3 - t E^2
         =1+3t+9t^2+11t^3+13t^4+8t^5+4t^6+t^7.

Crossing path RUUR.
Middle-path comparisons reduce to a>=c and g>=e. Write u=a-c>=0 and v=g-e>=0. The two corner comparisons become d+v>=b and d+u>=f. If d=1, both hold for arbitrary b,f, contributing t(1+t)^2 Q^2. If d=0, one pair together with its constrained outer variable contributes E+t(1+t)=1+t+2t^2; the two such choices are independent. Consequently
  G_RUUR=t(1+t)^2 Q^2 +(1+t+2t^2)^2
         =1+3t+9t^2+12t^3+14t^4+8t^5+4t^6+t^7.

Corner path RRUU.
The same-row competitor imposes b>=d. Split the three possibilities.
(b,d)=(1,1): the other comparisons reduce to a>=c,e>=g, with f arbitrary. Contribution t^2(1+t)Q^2.
(b,d)=(0,0): require a>=c,e>=g and (a-c)+(e-g)>=f. Contribution (1+t)Q^2-t E^2.
(b,d)=(1,0): all middle comparisons are automatic or follow from a+1+e>=c+f+g, so it remains only this inequality. For A=a+e and B=c+f+g, it is B<=A+1. Its generating polynomial is
  H=sum_{A=0}^2 binomial(2,A)t^A sum_{B=0}^{min(3,A+1)} binomial(3,B)t^B
   =1+5t+7t^2+9t^3+3t^4+t^5.
Including the weight t of b=1, this contribution is tH. Hence
  G_RRUU=(1+t)(1+t^2)Q^2-t E^2+tH
         =1+3t+11t^2+13t^3+17t^4+8t^5+4t^6+t^7.
Reflections and central reversal give the other three rows. This hand calculation verifies all 48 n=2 entries. It also shows G_RRUU-G_RURU=2t^2+2t^3+4t^4 and G_RRUU-G_RUUR=2t^2+t^3+3t^4, strictly positive for t>0; any assertion that these are legal source moves is separately controlled by the move-definition audit.
