1. Resolution of indexing and geometry

Let w be the 2n-letter R/U word and t_0=0<t_1<...<t_r=2n its run-end times.
The proper corners are t_1,...,t_{r-1}; the origin may also be used as t_0.
At time t_i, h_i=y-x=s_i.

A bump uses a proper corner k with both adjoining outer runs present:
2<=k<=r-2. Above the diagonal its incoming/outgoing letters are R/U and
h_k>=0; below they are U/R and h_k<=0. Reverse the word substring from
t_{k-1} through t_{k+1}. This exchanges the two constant-letter blocks and
merges them with the same-direction outer blocks, yielding exactly the
source's signed-run display. The lower bound k>=2 need not be independently
postulated in that display: k=1 is incompatible with the sign condition
since s_1=a_1. Weak diagonal equality is retained.

For a genuine unwind, choose 0<=l<k<=r-1 and k>=l+2. Above-side unwinding
requires h_k>0, h_l>0 if l>0, and h_i<0 for every l<i<k; l=0 replaces
the left endpoint condition by h_0=0. The below-side version changes all
signs. Reverse w[t_l:t_k], leaving every R and U letter unchanged. In run
notation this reverses a_{l+1},...,a_k, then merges same-sign neighbours.
For l>0 the number of runs decreases by two. For l=0 it decreases by ONE,
since no preceding run exists. This distinction matters for validation.

The last endpoint is not an extra boundary exception: h_r=0 cannot satisfy
the strict condition on h_k. Thus k<=r-1 is forced and a_{k+1} exists.
The source explicitly authorizes l=0 but does not authorize k=r or an
additional time-reversal closure. We do not add these operations. The word
reflection R<->U is the stated sign symmetry; whole-word reversal preserves
probabilities but need not preserve the literal directed single-step graph.

The empty-interior loophole k=l+1 is resolved as follows. The source's
geometric description requires a crossing excursion, while reversing the
corresponding one-run word does nothing. The run-merging display assumes
distinct segment indices; applying its collapsed-index version here can
count a segment twice and fails to describe that identity. We therefore
include all genuine crossing excursions, and omit the one-run identity.
This is an explicit contextual reading of the paper, not an author-confirmed
erratum. It is consistent with Figure 4's edge-order reversal, Lemma 9's
strict gain, and the statement that n=1 has no nontrivial moves. No further
definition ambiguity remains for this operational reading. Any theorem
must state this genuine-excursion convention rather than silently applying
the displayed algebra to empty index ranges.

Figure 3 labels a_1+a_3, a_2+a_4, a_5, a_6, matching reversal of blocks
a_2,a_3 at k=2; its caption rules out another concavity that crosses the
diagonal. Figure 4 labels a_1+a_5, a_4, a_3, a_6+a_2, matching reversal
of a_2,...,a_5 at (l,k)=(1,5). The independent implementation follows these
word operations, not a Euclidean line-reflection routine or R/U exchange.

3. Hand-derived n=1 and n=2 controls

For n=1 there are two independent nonendpoint weights A,B. A chosen path
is maximal exactly when A>=B, so F(p)=1-p(1-p), with raw counts [1,1,1].
There are no nontrivial legal moves.

For n=2 label the seven nonendpoint weights as
 a=(1,0), b=(2,0), c=(0,1), d=(1,1), e=(2,1), f=(0,2), g=(1,2).
The six path scores are a+b+e, a+d+e, a+d+g, c+d+e, c+d+g, c+f+g.
Set q=1-p, t=pq, A=1-t=p+q^2, B=p^2+q^2.

For staircase RURU, the maximality conditions simplify to
 a>=c, e>=g, d>=b, (a-c)+(e-g)+d>=f.
Conditioning on d gives
 F_RURU = p A^2 + q^2(A^2-p B^2) = A^3-p q^2 B^2.

For the crossing middle path RUUR, the conditions are a>=c, g>=e,
 b<=d+(g-e), f<=d+(a-c). Conditioning on d gives
 F_RUUR = p A^2 + q [q(p+B)]^2.

For corner RRUU, let alpha=c-a and beta=g-e. Each has probabilities
 P(-1)=t, P(0)=B, P(1)=t. Its conditions become
 b-d>=max(alpha,0)+max(beta,0), b-f>=alpha+beta.
The nine (alpha,beta) possibilities give
 F_RRUU = B^2(p+q^3) + (2tB+t^2)A + 2t^3 + 2t^2 B q.
The other three probabilities follow by lattice symmetry. Substitution
p=x/(1+x), multiplication by (1+x)^7, and expansion give raw-count rows:
 corner RRUU/UURR: [1,3,11,13,17,8,4,1];
 staircase RURU/URUR: [1,3,9,11,13,8,4,1];
 crossing RUUR/URRU: [1,3,9,12,14,8,4,1].
These are hand event decompositions, separately checked by coordinate-set
enumeration of all 128 assignments and grid dynamic programming. As an
additional algebra control, all three displayed polynomials (degree at most
seven) agree with their count-table expressions at the nine exact rational
values p=i/8, i=0,...,8; equality follows as a polynomial identity.

The four n=2 moves are RURU->RRUU, URUR->UURR (bumps),
RUUR->UURR, URRU->RRUU (boundary unwinds). Each has two-dot gain 2.
Their raw difference rows are respectively [0,0,2,2,4,0,0,0] for a bump
and [0,0,2,1,3,0,0,0] for an unwind, proving the full-p strict comparisons.

4. Audit of the n=4 exceptional coefficients and degree elevation

After the primary counts and independent DP replay were available, this
reviewer independently subtracted rows along every independently generated
edge. Exactly two n=4 edges have a negative raw coefficient:
 RUURUURR -> UURRUURR,
 URRURRUU -> RRUURRUU.
The first has source runs (-1,2,-1,2,-2). At (l,k)=(0,2), h_1=-1 and
h_2=1; reversing prefix RUU to UUR gives exactly UURRUURR, whose runs are
(2,-2,2,-2). It is therefore a genuine authorized boundary unwind, with
corner decrease one and mixed two-dot gain two. The second is its sign
reflection. Neither may be discarded to obtain coefficientwise positivity.

The common raw difference vector d_0,...,d_23 is
[0,0,2,-2,40,67,328,864,1530,2832,3783,3948,3629,2596,1347,536,
 204,94,44,12,1,0,0,0].

For q=1-p, multiplying sum d_j p^j q^(23-j) by p+q=1 gives degree-24
coefficients e_j=d_j+d_{j-1}, with out-of-range d's zero. They are
[0,0,2,0,38,107,395,1192,2394,4362,6615,7731,7577,6225,3943,
 1883,740,298,138,56,13,1,0,0,0].
Every entry is nonnegative and e_2=2>0. Hence the difference is strictly
positive for every 0<p<1. All the other 118 n=4 edges already have
nonnegative degree-23 coefficients and positive d_2. The same exact
elevation was independently checked along all 148 edges at n=2,3,4;
all have nonnegative elevated coefficients and positive e_2. This proof
needs no floating point sampling, root finding, or uncompleted subdivision.

The negative d_3 has a useful interpretation: conditional on exactly three
occupied nonendpoint sites, the first edge has probability difference
-2/binom(23,3)=-2/1771. Thus fixed-cardinality monotonicity is false, although
the Bernoulli-p comparison is true. This is not a counterexample to the
paper's Bernoulli conjecture.
