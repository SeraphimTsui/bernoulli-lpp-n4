McKeown and Nakajima, On the most likely geodesic in last passage percolation,
arXiv:2608.11378v2, 20 September 2026:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11378

Latest arXiv version page inspected 2026-10-30 still lists v2. Section2 defines
the operation and conjectures the independent Bernoulli path ordering. Theorem3,
equation6 and Section5 prove the ordering for small p; Lemma9 concerns the two-
dot contribution. The v2 paper is not an all-cardinality comparison theorem.
Inspected relevant source sections do not give the present fixed-three-site
parametric formula. This comparison is not an exhaustive search of all papers.

The relevant sections do not supply the specified path-pair formula. Full
implication testing against all their general cone results has not been done.

Earlier self-publication https://github.com/SeraphimTsui/bernoulli-lpp-n4,
manuscript Section4.1: n=4 fixed-three-free-sites example already recorded,
delta=-2/1771. The present contribution is the explicit analytic extension
to the specified family of paths for every n>=4; treating the n4 case alone
as new would be incorrect. The repository has not been updated with this draft.
